Fair questions

Is there a free trial?

No — and that's deliberate. Ivory Galleries is genuinely free to start (3 galleries, no card required), and IvoryOS is free for your first 3 clients. When you upgrade, it's month-to-month and you can cancel anytime from the billing portal — no countdown timers, no pressure.

What currency am I billed in?

Ivory Galleries subscriptions are billed in Australian dollars (A$). Unlock purchases your clients make are also charged in A$ — the price you set is the price they see.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes — manage everything from the billing portal inside your dashboard. Annual plans save two months on either product.

Who do I talk to if something breaks?

A human. Email wispersofthepastprints@gmail.com — the founder reads every message, usually the same day.
